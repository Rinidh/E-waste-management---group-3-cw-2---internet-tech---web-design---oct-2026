Project Background & Problem Scope

The rapid adoption of consumer electronics in the Greater Kampala Metropolitan Area (Kampala, Wakiso, and Mukono) has caused a massive surge in electronic waste (e-waste) [: 10]. Most electronic consumers in the urban center store broken, outdated gadgets indefinitely in their homes or dispose of them alongside mixed municipal household trash [: 10].

Environmental health analysis documented by the Center for Ecological Governance & Cultural Diversity (CECIC) Uganda notes that informal e-waste dismantling at major commercial hubs (such as downtown Kampala's Cornerstone Plaza) exposes urban communities to up to 1,000 toxic chemical substances, including heavy metals like lead and mercury [: 10]. This web platform bridges the gap between public awareness and physical waste-collection infrastructure.
